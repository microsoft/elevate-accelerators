# Educator and Leadership Use Cases

## Current data-backed scope

Use-case numbers are stable identifiers referenced by the `UseCases` columns in the Silver, Gold and calculation CSVs, not a reading order. Renumbering would break every one of those references, so a section may list its numbers out of sequence.

## Teacher

| Number | Use case description | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Prepare a class review.** Review the class's recorded assignments, their descriptions and point scales, and students' recorded submission states and scores. View assessment administrations separately by test label, subject and window. An optional AI summary can organize the existing evidence and questions for the teacher. | Help teachers prepare a class review and identify questions for discussion. |
| 2 | **Review a student's attendance and recorded work.** Select a student in the teacher's class, review attendance over a chosen period, and inspect the recorded states and grades of their assignments. An optional AI summary can highlight factual observations and questions for teacher review. | Help teachers decide what attendance and work observations to discuss with a student. |
| 6 | **Review a student's performance.** Bring together the student's graded coursework (points earned against points possible, by subject and over time) and their recorded assessment administrations with scores, so the teacher can prepare a performance conversation. | Help teachers see recorded achievement and its coverage, without inferring ability, predicting outcomes or ranking students. |
| 7 | **Plan and follow up a learner check-in.** See which learners met a visible rule (for example, 5 or more absences in 20 school days), what actions teachers logged for them, the follow-up notes, and the learner's attendance and missing work in equal windows before and after an action. An optional AI summary can draft check-in questions. | Help teachers and school leaders act early and look back honestly at what happened afterwards, without risk scores, predictions or claims that an action caused a change. |

## Head of school

| Number | Use case description | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Prepare a school attendance and records briefing.** Summarize attendance and its coverage for a selected week or period. Include an appendix of recorded assignment states and assessment administrations, with their actual dates and source context. An optional AI draft can organize the briefing and questions to investigate. | Help school leaders prepare meetings with a clear summary and questions to investigate. |
| 4 | **Review assignment schedules across classes.** View classes by subject, grade and teacher, and list their recorded assignment titles and base due dates for a selected period. An optional AI summary can point out dates to discuss when coordinating schedules. | Help the school coordinate assignment schedules across classes. |

## Head of district

| Number | Use case description | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | **Prepare a cross-school attendance briefing.** Compare school-level attendance and coverage for the same grade and period. Show distinct cohort sizes and separate present, known and expected student-days. An optional AI summary can describe attendance patterns and questions for school leaders without ranking schools. | Help district leaders discuss attendance patterns with schools, without ranking them. |
| 8 | **Compare school years.** See the schools on a map, how many learners are enrolled against the previous school year, how they arrived (continuing, transferred in, new) and where last year's learners went (continuing, transferred out, completed the final year group, left), and attendance and chronic absence against the same dates a year earlier. | Help district leaders see change between years and where to look next, without reading a cohort change as a school's performance or claiming why learners moved. |
